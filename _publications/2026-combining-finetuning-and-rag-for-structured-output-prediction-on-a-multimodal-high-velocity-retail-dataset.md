title: Combining Finetuning and RAG for Structured Output Prediction on a Multimodal High Velocity Retail Dataset
description: Rapidly changing product catalogs challenge static prediction models. This work studies how fine-tuning
  and retrieval-augmented generation can be combined to extract structured information from multimodal retail
  data.
why: Retail information changes quickly, so a prediction system must handle both new products and changing
  details. This work studies combining model fine-tuning with retrieval of relevant examples for extracting
  structured information. The practical motivation is to make specialized knowledge and up-to-date context
  work together in one pipeline.
