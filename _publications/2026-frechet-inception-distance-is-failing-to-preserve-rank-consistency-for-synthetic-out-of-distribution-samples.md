  This work examines rank consistency for synthetic out-of-distribution samples, focusing attention on the
  reliability of generator evaluation.

  whether a widely used score keeps that ordering trustworthy when the images come from unfamiliar distributions.
  The question matters because an unreliable ranking can direct development toward the wrong model.
